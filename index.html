ABOUT ME

I am a creative Graphic Designer and Illustrator with a strong passion for visual storytelling, illustration, and artistic expression.

My artistic journey combines traditional drawing, digital illustration, and graphic design, allowing me to explore different styles, materials, textures, and visual moods.

I enjoy creating artwork that does more than simply look beautiful — I want it to communicate a feeling, tell a story, and leave a lasting impression.

My work includes character design, digital illustration, traditional artwork, visual compositions, branding, and social media design.

I am always looking for new ways to develop my artistic language and transform ideas into meaningful visual concepts.

MY CREATIVE APPROACH

I believe that every design has a story.

My approach begins with understanding the idea, emotion, and message behind a project. From there, I explore composition, color, lighting, texture, typography, and visual elements to create a cohesive result.

I pay particular attention to:

  • Creative concepts
  • Composition & visual balance
  • Rich textures
  • Lighting & atmosphere
  • Character expression
  • Color harmony
  • Visual storytelling
  • Attention to detail

CHARACTER DESIGN

Character design is one of the areas I enjoy exploring the most.

I focus on creating characters with distinctive personalities, expressions, shapes, and visual identities.

For me, a successful character is not only visually appealing — it should feel alive and communicate something about its personality.

DIGITAL ILLUSTRATION

I enjoy creating digital artwork with different moods, textures, lighting conditions, and atmospheres.

I particularly love combining imaginative ideas with strong visual composition to create illustrations that feel expressive and immersive.

TRADITIONAL ART

Traditional drawing is an important part of my artistic foundation.

Working with different materials allows me to experiment with texture, value, color, and expressive marks.

I work with a variety of traditional media, including pencil, charcoal, watercolor, gouache, markers, acrylic, and oil.

GRAPHIC DESIGN

My graphic design work focuses on creating visual solutions that combine creativity with clarity.

I enjoy developing identities, posters, social media content, packaging concepts, and advertising visuals while maintaining consistency between the concept and the final design.
